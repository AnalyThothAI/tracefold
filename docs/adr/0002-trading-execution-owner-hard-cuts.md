# Keep one execution owner, and delete the ones it replaced

Trading has had four execution owners. Each replacement was a hard cut rather than a second path, and each cut deleted its predecessor's tables, commands and vocabulary instead of leaving them queryable. This record exists so an operator meeting an old word in a log, a backup or a closed issue can place it; nothing here describes a current table, writer, route or command. `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` describes what runs today, and disagreement between the two is a defect in that document, not here.

## The owners, in order

**The DSPy Trading program (retired by #331).** A model decided entry, a `regime` column and a `program_version` / `program_sha256` / `program_output` triple on `trading_cases` recorded what it produced, and a `funnel` JSONB document on `trading_runtime_state` counted one entry per re-read of the same frame and reset on the UTC day key, so its magnitudes were a function of the poll interval. `#331` replaced the model with one pure deterministic policy, the funnel with the admission ledger, and `oi_momentum_v1`'s OI/price quadrant with the arithmetic band `market_context.py` still computes. `20260829_0325` dropped `funnel`, `dspy_calls_today` and `day_key`; `20260903_0355` dropped the four Case columns.

**Capital authority and `TradeIntent` (retired by #433-C).** Execution permission was `active capability snapshot − canonical blacklist`: a content-addressed snapshot joined the public News Binance-perp projection with every instrument the pinned Nautilus Binance USD-M provider returned, partitioning the union into executable instruments with frozen native identity, increments, precision, minimums and stop support, or excluded instruments with one closed reason. Only a Case whose frozen decision was `long`, whose frozen snapshot digest named the active pointer, and whose complete `InstrumentRef` matched an included capability could emit a `TradeIntent`. `trading.order.fixed_notional_usd` (`0 < value <= 10`) was the only operator execution value; the image owned Demo, NETTING one-way, long-only 1x market entry, a 60 s Intent TTL, a fixed-quantity `STOP_MARKET` 200 bps below entry, a 180 s maximum hold, 25 bps entry drift, 30 bps spread, and at most one nonterminal Intent globally. `LaneStorage.commit_long_decision` and then `commit_capital_disposition` were its deep seams, and `trading_cases.capital_disposition` / `capital_reason` were where the second of those wrote its answer. The cutover migrations `20260828_0316`, `20260828_0317`, `20260828_0320`, `20260829_0327`, `20260829_0329` and `20260830_0331` each required control `PAUSED` with no nonterminal Intent, because a pre-quote-authority fence cannot reconstruct the quote or quantity that authorized it. `20260901_0341` froze all 22 of those tables read-only, `20260901_0347` dropped them, and their 390 archived rows are in `~/.tracefold/backups/pre-0347-retired-trading-tables-20260901.sql`.

**The dormant Signal transport (#433-A/B).** The baseline `20260831_0340` carried a Signal / OperatorIntent / Observation transport and a `LISTEN/NOTIFY` wake hint that no process consumed, plus a separate `integrations.nautilus.oi_runtime` package with no TradingNode behind it. `20260901_0341` made `TradeSignalV1` the sole handoff and bound it one-to-one to `SIGNAL_EMITTED`. The transport was never a second owner; it was the current one, not yet started.

**The Nautilus OI Runtime (current, activated by #433-E).** This is the owner `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` describes. Its pre-change owner matrix and concurrency measurements — taken before #475 altered any cadence, and explicitly recording provider event-loop, inbound-rate and rate-limit evidence as unobserved until an active Demo Runtime can produce it — are in [`../research/oi-runtime-pr0-baseline-2026-09-01.md`](../research/oi-runtime-pr0-baseline-2026-09-01.md).

## Words an old row may still carry

`trading_cases.state` held `POLICY_REJECTED` (the pre-#433 writer's single word for both "the policy said no" and "the lane could not decide"), `INTENT_EMITTED` (a `TradeIntent` existed) and `ORDER_PREPARED` (the retired Paper/OpenTrade writer had built an order). `trading_candidate_gate_decisions.status` held `RESEARCH_ONLY` for a venue that could be studied and not traded, and its `stage` held `capability`, `catalog` and `routing` from the capability and venue-routing gates. `20260903_0355` removed all seven from their CHECKs and refuses to run while a row still uses one; the operator archives and deletes those rows first, and `docs/MIGRATIONS.md` carries the commands. Admission also once wrote `source_generation_mismatch`, `provider_symbol_*`, `blacklisted`, `catalog_absent`, `rank_above_limit`, `cooldown`, `active_underlying` and `case_in_flight`; `#348` retired the selectivity rules (selectivity belongs to the policy, and a re-entry delay is what a lane needs only when several positions can be open at once) and `#510 PR-4` retired the News-generation rules with the News identity fields they read.

## Why the numbering skips

`#449` squashed revisions `20260818_0275` through `20260831_0339` into the reviewed baseline `20260831_0340`, which is both the Alembic root and, at the time, the head. Migration numbers below `0340` therefore name revisions that exist only in Git history and in the recorded pre-cut image; no supported database will apply one again. `docs/MIGRATIONS.md` records the operator authorization for that exception.

## What none of this licenses

No compatibility path, dual read, forwarding module or "legacy" branch survives any of these cuts, and none may be reintroduced to make an archived row readable by current code. An archived row is read by restoring its dump into a scratch database.
